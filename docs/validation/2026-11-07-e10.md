## What it shows

- **Twelve cursors with skills now finish sooner too.** Over the nine diagrams the median finish was 118 s against 214 s, about 1.8× sooner, at about 1.25× the tokens. In base and slow the token count was even lower than one cursor's.
  - There was one failure: on big 801 the agent stopped at 91%.
  - One run had a long tail: big 803 finished only at 490 s.
  - E9 had shown no gain in finishing time. The difference between rounds is large with three diagrams per cell.
- **Shifting down makes the run reliable but slow.** The fresh one-cursor finisher completed every diagram, including the one skills-multi left at 91%. But it first has to re-read the reference and the board, which took 50–300 s and many tokens even when only one or two items were missing.
  - The bulk itself was fastest with the shift arm (median 72 s to 90%), because the agent knew it could hand off.
  - A finisher that inherits the board state as text, instead of starting from pictures, is the obvious next step.
- **Bigger and slower tasks favour many cursors**, as expected, though not quite as much as preregistered: 1.8× and 1.9× to 90%.
