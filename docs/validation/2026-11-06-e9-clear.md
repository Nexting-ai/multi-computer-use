## What it shows

- **On clear pictures, everyone gets it right.** Every arm completed every diagram (multi once reached 96%). The errors in E7 and E8 came mostly from the ambiguous references.
- **Twelve cursors do the bulk about 1.6× faster.** They reach 90% in about 77 s against 124 s for one cursor. One cursor spent about two thirds of its time executing actions (77–82 s executing against 27–40 s thinking), so hands that work in parallel save real time.
- **But they don't finish sooner.** The median time to 100% was worse (166–186 s against 130 s). Each review-and-fix decision about 12 cursors makes the model think longer, so the last few items took one or two minutes. One run finished clearly faster: 702 skills-multi, complete in 80 s against 129 s.
- **The cost is about 2× the tokens** (0.26M against 0.12M).
- **Skills alone did not speed one cursor up.** They cut decisions 6× (9 against 53), but the cursor still had to perform every action. They pay off only together with more hands, as in skills-multi: the fewest decisions (4–11), and the only arm always complete.
