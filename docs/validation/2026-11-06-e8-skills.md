## A flaw found after the runs: the reference pictures were ambiguous

The reference diagrams in E7 and E8 drew every arrow from box centre to box centre. After rendering:
- many arrows crossed the labels;
- arrows in a row ran end to end through several boxes;
- a few arrows were drawn toward empty space.

The wrong arrows were largely the same across arms (for example "Checkout→Auth" on 702 in four separate runs), so they reflect the pictures as much as the agents.

Accuracy in E7 and E8 is therefore confounded and is **not** used for conclusions. Speed, decisions, tokens and the split between thinking and execution are unaffected.

The references are fixed (`lab/board/diagram.ts`: arrows run edge to edge, with solid heads and a plain font). E9 repeats the comparison on the fixed pictures.

## What still holds

- **Skills cut decisions 3–8×** (16–45 against 64–134) and usually cut tokens (702: 0.34M against 0.63M). The agents defined the right skills themselves: a box with a label, and an arrow.
- **Skills with 12 cursors were fastest to 50%** (82–95 s against 126–200 s) with the fewest decisions (5–21).
